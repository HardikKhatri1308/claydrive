Privacy Policy

Last updated: 11 November 2026

ClayDrive ("the app") is a personal macOS app that shows your Google Drive as a drive in Finder. This policy explains what data the app uses and how.

Data the app accesses

  • Google Drive files and folders of the account you sign in with, so they can be listed, opened, uploaded, renamed and deleted from Finder at your request.
  • Your Drive storage quota (space used and free), shown in the app's menu.
  • An access token issued by Google when you sign in. Your Google password is never seen or stored by the app.

How the data is used

  • Data is used only to provide the drive in Finder on your own Mac.
  • All processing happens locally on your Mac. The app has no servers, and no data is sent to the developer or any third party. Your files travel only between your Mac and Google.
  • No analytics, tracking, advertising or profiling.

Storage on your Mac

  • Parts of files you recently opened are cached in ~/Library/Caches/ClayDrive, up to a size limit you choose. You can delete this folder at any time.
  • The Google access token is stored in the local rclone configuration file on your Mac (~/.config/rclone/rclone.conf).
  • A local log file is kept at ~/Library/Logs/ClayDrive.log for troubleshooting. It never leaves your Mac.

Sharing

The app does not sell, share, transfer or disclose Google user data to anyone. It does not use Google user data to develop, improve or train AI or machine learning models.

Google API Services User Data Policy

ClayDrive's use and transfer of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.

Removing access

  • Revoke ClayDrive's access at any time at myaccount.google.com/permissions.
  • Delete the app, ~/Library/Caches/ClayDrive and the claydrive entry in ~/.config/rclone/rclone.conf to remove all local data.

Changes

If this policy changes, the updated version will be posted on this page with a new date.
