Your Google Drive, as a drive on your Mac.

ClayDrive is a small personal macOS menu bar app. It shows the Google Drive of the person using it as a drive in Finder, and streams files on demand so they take almost no space on the Mac.

How it works
  • You sign in with your own Google account. ClayDrive never sees your password.
  • Files open straight from your Google Drive. Only the parts you open are downloaded and cached on your Mac.
  • Files you add to the ClayDrive drive are uploaded to your own Google Drive.
  • Everything runs on your Mac. There are no ClayDrive servers.

Who it is for

ClayDrive is a personal project for its developer's own use. It is not sold or offered as a public service.
